Customer Retention Strategy

Critical Risk Customers
- Customers with very high churn probability should receive immediate attention.
- High-value customers should receive priority retention treatment.
- Customers with negative sentiment should receive proactive support.
- Customers showing payment failures should be contacted to resolve billing issues.

High Value Customers
- High-value customers should receive personalized engagement.
- Loyalty benefits can be considered for customers with strong engagement.
- Retention offers should be proportional to the customer's potential revenue impact.

Negative Sentiment
- Customers with repeated negative support interactions should receive customer experience follow-up.
- Support teams should prioritize unresolved or repeated customer complaints.

Low Engagement
- Customers with declining logins or usage should receive personalized engagement campaigns.
- Educational content and product guidance can be used to improve product adoption.

Retention Principle
- Prioritize customers using a combination of churn risk, customer value, engagement, sentiment, and support behavior.
