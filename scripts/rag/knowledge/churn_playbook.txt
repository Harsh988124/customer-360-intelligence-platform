Churn Risk Playbook

High Churn Risk
- Review the customer's recent activity.
- Review support history and sentiment.
- Review payment failures.
- Review customer value before selecting an intervention.
- Use personalized retention communication for appropriate customers.

Critical Churn Risk
- Prioritize immediate intervention.
- High-value critical customers should receive high-touch retention.
- Investigate customer experience problems.
- Monitor the customer after intervention.

Medium Churn Risk
- Use targeted engagement.
- Monitor changes in usage and support activity.
- Consider personalized communication.

Low Churn Risk
- Continue normal customer engagement.
- Monitor for changes in behavior.
